Nomadology's concepts, and how they have moved

A concept, as Deleuze often put it, is the movement behind its name. Indeed, these names have moved a great deal. Each row gives a concept of the two plateaus in plain words, the field from which it came, where it was worked out before 1980 (CERFI's journal Recherches in 1972-73, Deleuze's seminars at Vincennes in 1979-80), and how it has been taken up since. R13 is Recherches 13 (December 1973), cited by its printed pages.

Code, overcoding, axiomatic427, 448-54, 461-62A code relates qualified elements within a domain, overcoding subordinates codes to a higher unity, and an axiomatic treats unqualified flows directlyLévi-Strauss's codes of exchange, the genetic code (Jacob and Monod), Hjelmslev, and set theoryGuattari in 1973: "public facilities are the axioms of capital ... no intrinsic coding" R13 131Code heard as computer code, though most of what now goes by that name is axiomatic
