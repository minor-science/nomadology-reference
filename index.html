Nomadology: reference sheets for A Thousand Plateaus, plateaus 12 and 13NomadologyReference sheets for A Thousand Plateaus, plateaus 12 and 13

Working references for the two plateaus in which Deleuze and Guattari set out nomadology: "1227: Treatise on Nomadology - The War Machine" and "7000 B.C.: Apparatus of Capture." They were made while writing a response to John Protevi's Regimes of Violence: Toward a Political Anthropology (Minnesota, 2025), which takes up both plateaus and adds a figure of its own, the maroon.

Page numbers without a prefix refer to Brian Massumi's translation (Minnesota, 1987); Mille plateaux (Minuit, 1980) is cited where the French wording counts, while RoV marks Protevi. Each sheet has a web version and a printable PDF.

A second tab regroups the same material by theme, from royal and nomad science and the three spaces to number, the war machine, and weapons set against tools.

Concepts

  • [2]430-37

    The five machinic processes

    "We define social formations by machinic processes and not by modes of production" (435): mechanisms of prevention-anticipation, war machines, apparatuses of capture, polarization in towns, and ecumenical or international organization, each in coexistence with the others (430-37). A second version adds Protevi's marronage as a column.

    WebWith marronagePDF

  • [3]361-74

    Royal science and nomad science

    Proposition IV of the Treatise (361-74) sets royal, theorematic science against minor, problematic science (hydraulic models, smooth space, vague essences) along a "constantly shifting borderline" (367). On its back page, questions that anthropology, archaeology, and history ask from the side of the State become questions for a nomadology.

    WebPDF

  • [4]459

    The State's three forms

    Proposition XIII of Apparatus of Capture, the State's forms, set out question by question from the archaic imperial State (the Urstaat) through the evolved States to the modern nation-States (459). Its questions run from regimes of signs and forms of law to what each form does with the war machine.

    WebPDF

  • [5]concepts

    Nomadology's concepts, and how they have moved

    A concordance of the plateaus' concepts (nomos, the war machine, the apparatus of capture, the right of use and others) in plain words, with the field in which each began, where it was worked out before 1980 (CERFI's Recherches of 1972-73, Deleuze's Vincennes seminars of 1979-80), and how it has been taken up since.

    After 1980: what the record does to nomadology's claims

    Claim by claim, the archaeology, anthropology and history since the plateaus that confirm, complicate or dispute them, along with the literatures since 1980 placed on a single scale and the scholarship on nomadology by strand.
