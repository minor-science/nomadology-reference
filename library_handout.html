The plateaus' library

Sources of plateaus 12-13 · front

Deleuze and Guattari built nomadology out of other people's research: myth, anthropology, archaeology, history, mathematics, military theory, and political economy, with literature throughout. Their notes to the two plateaus (ATP 552-71) cite about a hundred works, grouped here by field, each with what it gave the plateaus. Sources marked against are ones they dispute or correct, which is part of the method too. Yet every field here has moved in the fifty years since.

The workshop around the plateaus

Library · back

Much of the two plateaus was worked out aloud first, in the 1979-80 seminar at Vincennes on "Apparatuses of Capture and War Machines", thirteen sessions between November and March, with students (Éric Alliez, Georges Comtesse, Richard Pinhas, Claire Parnet) intervening. The table matches each session to the pages it develops; the list below names the other places the ideas live. Every passage, with verified quotations, is in work/context/context_12_13.md and under "Elsewhere" on the web reference.
