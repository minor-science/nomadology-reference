The State's three forms

Deleuze and Guattari distinguish "three major forms" of the State (459). In fact, they are not stages, since each form is in "resonance" with the others, and all of them "exhibit a unity of composition in spite of their differences in organization and development" (459). The sheet opens the single column that The five machinic processes gives to capture. Brackets join the forms where Deleuze and Guattari set out a relation between them.

The axiomatic today: seven givens

In the present, as D&G see it, "capitalism and present-day politics are an axiomatic in the literal sense," which is why "nothing is played out in advance" (461). They set out "a summary sketch of the 'givens'" in seven parts. The last column records where, by their own account, struggle enters.

GivenATPWhat Deleuze and Guattari sayWhere struggle enters
1. Addition, subtraction461-63Axioms are "operative statements." Social democracy adds them (Keynes and the New Deal as "axiom laboratories"), totalitarianism subtracts them, as in "the minimum State of anarcho-capitalism (cf. Chile)""It would be an error to take a disinterested stance toward struggle on the level of the axioms" 463
2. Saturation463-64Capitalism "has no laws but immanent ones." It confronts its own limits and displaces them at once (oil, nuclear power)Local struggles must "directly target national and international axioms, at the precise point of their insertion" 464
3. Models, isomorphy464-66All States are models of realization: isomorphic at the center, heteromorphic in the socialist East, polymorphic at the peripheryIt "would be absurd" to think popular movements condemned in advance, or all States "equivalent and homogeneous" 466
4. Power466-67A "power of the continuum" in the military-industrial complexes, where "peace ... technologically frees the unlimited material process of total war"The same capital "continually recreate[s] unexpected possibilities for counterattack" 422
5. The included middle468-69The center needs a periphery, and "internal Third Worlds" rise inside the center, with labor split between intensive surplus labor and "erratic" workNegri's "internal margin," which "tends increasingly to merge the students with the emarginati" 469
