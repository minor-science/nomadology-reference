I. Counts and observations

  • Headed statements: 3 Axioms (351, 380, 415), 14 Propositions (I-IX in plateau 12, X-XIV in plateau 13), 3 Problems (356, 374, 403), 7 givens under Proposition XIV. Axiom II is withdrawn at 422.
  • Plateau 12 argues by attestation and consequence (Propositions I-IV "attested," V-VIII "necessarily"). Plateau 13 argues by schema and model (the 1-2-3 schema, Schmitt's A-D, the trinity formula, the seven givens).
  • The word "operative" marks nomad geometry (364, 368, 389: "a minor, operative geometry"), Schmitt's schema (446), and the axioms of capital (461). Minor science and the axiomatic share it.
  • "Relations internal to the number" names the war machine's power (392) and the minority (469). The numbering number of plateau 12 returns as the nondenumerable set of plateau 13.
  • Warding off and anticipating (Clastres) runs from Problem I (356) through the convergent wave (431), the last (439), the towns against capitalism (434), and capitalism against its own limits (437, 463).
  • "Not better, just different" (372) and "The question is not one of good or bad but of specificity" (390) are the text's two refusals of value judgment between the poles. "Never believe that a smooth space will suffice to save us" is not in these plateaus (it is 500, plateau 14).

Plateau 12. Axiom I, Proposition I 351-355

  • Guattari seminar Later version (ATP 351-352). Two years after the plateau, Guattari moves the war machine into his schizoanalytic cartography as one of four positions. “La machine de guerre, c’est le fait qu’un territoire fonctionne comme machine par le détour de rapports a-signifiants et de points-signes machiniques sans rapport de signification” ("The war machine is the fact that a territory functions as a machine by way of a-signifying relations and machinic point-signs without a relation of signification"). Its opposite: “un territoire qui fonctionne avec un univers en empruntant le rapport de signification est une machine de pouvoir” ("a territory that functions with a universe by borrowing the relation of signification is a machine of power"). Source: guattari-seminars/1982-01-26_26-01-1982-les-formations-du-noyau-dagencement.txt, Guattari's seminar, 26 Jan 1982, « Les formations du noyau d'agencement », PDF 7.
  • Dialogues Earlier version (ATP 351-361). Propositions I-III in a single paragraph, three years before the plateau: “Like Felix, we assume that the war-machine has a nature and origin quite different from that of the apparatus of the State.” The nomads, arithmetic organization, and open space are already there, and minor science too: “even when the war-machine is related to a geometry, it is a quite different geometry, a sort of Archimedean geometry, a geometry of 'problems', and not of 'theorems' like Euclid's”. Becomings and the secret: “cf. the secret as the invention of the war-machine, as opposed to the 'publicity' of the despot or the man of the State”. Dumézil's eccentric warrior, de Heusch's war machine « from outside », and Clastres's war against the State all appear, with « Genghis Khan and the emperor of China » and Moses's organization of flight. Source: flight/dialogues.txt, Dialogues, « Many Politics », pp. 141-42 (PDF 156-57).
  • Seminar 79-80 s.1 Develops (ATP 351-380). The hypothesis of Axiom I and Axiom II, restated as a response: “if it is true that the nomads invented the war machine, it is in specific environments, in determinate conditions and directed precisely against State apparatuses; and that the war machine was a kind of riposte, of parade, with regard to the State apparatus”. Armies are kept apart from the start: the nomads did not invent armies. Source: purdue/ATP-V-79-80-Complete-English.txt, seminar 1979-80, session 1 (6 Nov 1979), PDF 3.
  • Dumézil Source (ATP 352). The warrior who « unbinds » of plateau 12 (n. 4, 352): the god of the men's societies figures « as the god who unfetters » Varuna's “(legally) bound victims; for the warrior and the sorcerer alike or, on another level, the soldier and the policeman, make inroads when necessary on the life and liberty of their fellow man”, each by procedures the other finds repugnant. The soldier and the policeman, already here, prepare the two violences of 448. Source: capture/dumezil_mitra_varuna.txt, Mitra-Varuna, PDF 108.
  • Seminar 79-80 s.2 Develops (ATP 353-354). De Heusch's public Nkongolo and secret Mbidi (n. 6) become a principle: in the archaic empire « the despot eats in public, sleeps in public ». “The secret is born with the war machine. No war machine, no secret.” The despot's mask is for showing: “It is the public mask, not the mask of the secret.” The State secret is late, from the moment States appropriate war machines. Source: purdue/ATP-V-79-80-Complete-English.txt, seminar 1979-80, session 2 (13 Nov 1979), PDF 32-33.
  • Seminar 1977 Earlier version (ATP 355-356). « Goethe et Hegel, penseurs d'Etat » against Kleist is first a matter of time and the subject: “I tried to oppose Kleist to Goethe; Kleist, subject formation, he could care less [se fout] completely. It's not his affair, his affair is a story of speeds and slownesses.” It comes in a discussion of pulsed and non-pulsed time (Boulez), which ties Kleist's catatonias and précipitations to smooth time. Source: purdue/A-Thousand-Plateaus-II-Two-Forms-of-Segm/ATP-II-03-May-8-1977-English.txt, seminar ATP II, 8 May 1977, PDF 9.

It’s like a means of warding off the accumulation of wealth.” Hence “necessarily, mechanisms of anticipation – warding off” [anticipation – conjuration]. This is the root of Proposition XI's « conjurer, c'est aussi anticiper ». Source: purdue/ATP-V-79-80-Complete-English.txt, seminar 1979-80, session 3 (20 Nov 1979), PDF 49-51.

Section 5 (« Undecidable propositions », 471-73): “the capitalist axiomatic, or capitalism as axiomatic, does not cease generating undecidable propositions”. An undecidable is « an included third ». Capital tries two ways to recover them, adding axioms and extermination, and neither works: “the camps multiply the people locked up there rather than suppress them”, as organized hunger multiplies the hungry. The four undecidable flows, from McNamara's World Bank speeches: “flows of raw materials, flows of food materials, flows of population, flows of urbanization-slums”. The method: “a system or a social field is not defined by its contradictions”, but by its lines of flight. Connections, not conjugations, are the revolutionary possibility.
