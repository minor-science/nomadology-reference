Where the name came from

Nomadology, the name · front

The word "nomadology" has more roots than the nomads. In Greek, the pasture came before both the law and any people, and Deleuze took his nomos from that philology in 1968, long before the steppe. In A Thousand Plateaus the word first names a lack, and later what monadology turns into once its units open. Each row gives a source and what the word named there, along with what it brings to the name.

What nomadology is not, and other names

Nomadology, the name · back

Several approaches outside Deleuze and Guattari's orbit look like nomadology from a distance, and each shares something with it. They are useful as contrasts, since what separates them from nomadology is usually where they start, from a people to be given back its history or from a history to be told otherwise.

ApproachWorksWhat it shares with nomadologyWhere nomadology differs
GenealogyFoucault, "Nietzsche, Genealogy, History" (1971)Against origins and against history told from above. "Effective history" places us "among countless lost events, without a landmark or a point of reference" (155)Genealogy remains a kind of history. Nomadology is "the opposite of a history" 23 and studies processes that coexist in every formation 437
History of the people left outWolf, Europe and the People without History (1982)Refuses the "primitive contemporaries" (25) who were said to have no history. Wolf's world is no "global pool hall" of bounded societies (6)Wolf puts "the people to whom history has been denied" into "the same historical trajectory" (23). Nomadology doesn't seek a place in history for them. It questions the sedentary point of view from which history is written
Silences of the archiveTrouillot, Silencing the Past (1995)Power enters the record at every stage, from making sources and archives to making narrativesTrouillot asks what a record silences. Nomadology also asks what the State converted and then presented as its own invention
Legibility and state evasionScott, Seeing Like a State (1998), The Art of Not Being Governed (2009), Against the Grain (2017)Counting as a way of making a population legible, and peoples who live beyond the State's reach, from Zomia to "The Golden Age of the Barbarians"Scott's units are States and the peoples who escape them. Nomadology names no people. Its processes run inside States as well as outside them
A history of freedomsGraeber and Wengrow, The Dawn of Everything (2021)Against stages, they find seasonal changes of social form and societies that chose against hierarchyTheirs is still an account of what past peoples chose. Nomadology asks how a formation wards off a threshold it has never crossed 437-39
Studies of pastoral nomadsKhazanov, Nomads and the Outside World (1983), Lindner, "What Was a Nomadic Tribe?" (1982)The steppe itself. Lindner finds the tribe was "a political organism open to all who were willing to subordinate themselves to its chief" (701)These fields study nomads as peoples and economies. The plateaus define the nomad by a way of inhabiting space, not by a people or an economy 380-81
Mobility studiesSheller and Urry, "The New Mobilities Paradigm" (2006)Takes movement seriously, against theories of society built on settlementThe nomad is "he who does not move" 381. What nomadology studies is a distribution in space, not travel
Nomos as appropriationSchmitt, The Nomos of the Earth (1950)The same Greek word, and the same concern with how the earth is orderedSchmitt's nomos begins with taking and dividing land. Nomadology begins from the pasture that no one divides 557 n. 51

Names already in use

  • Challenger's six others. Rhizomatics, stratoanalysis, schizoanalysis, micropolitics, pragmatics, the science of multiplicities 43
  • Minor science, nomad science. The science that follows its material, against royal science 361-74. It is the name of this site
  • Nomad thought. The title of Deleuze's 1972 talk, and of the thought set against the State form 376-80
  • Mobile pastoralism. What steppe research now calls the way of life once named nomadism (Humphrey and Sneath). It names an economy, not a method

What a new name would need

  1. Name no people, because a people was never the root
  2. Keep distribution without division, the pasture no one parcels out
  3. Keep the opened unit and the local link, step by step
  4. Put politics before being, as Mille plateaux does ("avant l'être, il y a la politique," "before being, there is politics," Mille plateaux 249)
  5. Avoid ending in a law or a logos, or make that ending visible on purpose
  6. Be sayable outside the university

Where one could come from

  • From the opened monad. Named for the local, step-by-step link that replaces the closed unit 494, 574 n. 27
  • From nem-. Named for distributing rather than dividing, with the people left out
  • From the method. Following, the problematic, rigor without exactness. This is closest to minor science
  • From Challenger's list. Take one of the aliases, or add an eighth, as the joke allows 43
  • Keep the name and turn it. Tell the word's own history, in which a habitat without limits was later claimed as a division

Our own observation, which Deleuze and Guattari do not make, is that "nomado-logy" puts a logos over the nomos. Plateau 12 calls this move "the triumph of the logos or the law over the nomos" 373.
