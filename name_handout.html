Where the name came from

Nomadology, the name · front

As its spelling suggests, Deleuze and Guattari built the word from two parts. The first, "nomad-," reaches back through Laroche's philology to the Greek root nem-, and through Deleuze's own work to the nomad as a figure of thought, to which the steppe historians then gave peoples. The second, "-ology," comes from Leibniz's Monadology, though Deleuze and Guattari also set it against "a history," which is to say against the sedentary point of view from which history is written. Each row gives a source they used and what it gives the name.

In brief. The word was not in the 1977 list of names for the project (Dialogues 125), and Deleuze never said it in the 1979-80 seminar on capture and war machines. It arrived with the book in 1980, in four places at once: as something lacking, "the opposite of a history" 23, as one of Professor Challenger's aliases 43, as the title of plateau 12, and in the note where a monadology of opened units "should be superseded by a 'nomadology'" 574 n. 27. Its parts, however, are older: "nomad" goes back through Deleuze's 1972 talk and his 1968 "nomadic distribution" to Laroche's Greek. The form of the word comes from Leibniz. We have found no earlier use of "nomadology."

Other names

Nomadology, the name · names

Below are the names already in use and what a new name would need. Each route then ends on a suggested name. Apart from geophilosophy, which is Deleuze and Guattari's own, we have found none of them in use.

Names already in use

  • Challenger's seven names. Rhizomatics, stratoanalysis, schizoanalysis, nomadology, micropolitics, pragmatics, the science of multiplicities 43
  • Geophilosophy. Deleuze and Guattari's own later name, for a philosophy that arises from "a geography rather than a historiography" (What Is Philosophy? 96). They credit Nietzsche with founding it (102)
  • The 1977 aliases. Schizoanalysis, micro-politics, pragmatics, diagrammatism, rhizomatics, cartography (Dialogues 125). Nomadology joined the series in 1980, and diagrammatism and cartography left it
  • Geopoetics. The name Kenneth White has used since the late 1970s for "cette densification de la géographie" ("this densification of geography") that the nomad must invent (L'Esprit nomade 11, 272-94). A rival name in use, with the nomad kept outside politics
  • Minor science, nomad science. The science that follows its material, against royal science 361-74. It is the name of this site
  • Nomad thought. The title of Deleuze's 1972 talk, and of the thought set against the State form 376-80
  • Mobile pastoralism. What steppe research now calls the way of life once named nomadism (Humphrey and Sneath). It names an economy, not a method

What a new name would need

  1. It should not name a people. In Greek the word named a way of life, "not a nation" (Laroche 121)
  2. It should keep the sense of distribution without division into shares, which plateau 12 takes from the Greek nomos 380
  3. It should keep the opened monad of the Riemann note, in which each unit has direct relations with its neighbors 574 n. 27
  4. It should put politics before being, as Guattari did first ("before Being there is politics," Dialogues 17) and A Thousand Plateaus repeats 203
  5. If it ends in "-logy," it should say why, given the observation below
  6. It should make sense to readers outside philosophy

Where one could come from

  • From nem-. A name for distribution without division into shares 380. Suggested: Nemology. The root nem- with "-logy." It also stays clear of "nomology," which already means the science of laws
  • From the nomad as a figure. A name for a way of life without a people, like Laroche's adjective nomádes 121, or for nomad thought ("Nomad Thought," 1972). Suggested: Nomadics. Formed like "politics" or "ethics," so that it names a practice and no science of nomads
  • From the monad. A name for the monad once it is opened and joined to its neighbors 574 n. 27. Suggested: Open monadology. Leibniz's word kept, minus the closure the Riemann note removes
  • From the opposite of a history. A name for geography against history 23. Suggested: Geophilosophy. Deleuze and Guattari's own name from 1991, already in use (What Is Philosophy? 95-96)
  • From the word itself. An eighth name for Challenger's list 43, or "nomadology" kept with its history told. Suggested: Nemesiology. From the same root comes nemesis, the anger of the gods at anyone who takes more than a share. Such a name would keep the punishment in view, which is to say the very thing a distribution without shares escapes

Our own observation, which Deleuze and Guattari do not make, is that "nomado-logy" puts a logos over the nomos. Plateau 12 calls this move "the triumph of the logos or the law over the nomos" 373.
