Where the name came from

Nomadology, the name · front

The word "nomadology" gathers several threads, and nomos is only one of them. Some begin from history and thought, others from the monad, the steppe or the pasture, and a last thread follows the word itself. Each thread runs through its sources in order. The letters beside a thread's name show which others it meets.

In brief. The word was not in the 1977 list of names for the project (Dialogues 125), and Deleuze never said it in the 1979-80 seminar on capture and war machines (French and English transcripts). It arrived with the book in 1980, in four places at once: as something lacking, "the opposite of a history" 23, as one of Professor Challenger's aliases 43, as the title of plateau 12, and in the note where a monadology of opened units "should be superseded by a 'nomadology'" 574 n. 27. The 1986 Semiotext(e) booklet printed plateau 12 alone and made the word the English name for the treatise. By 1988 interviewers put it in quotation marks, and Deleuze answered that the nomads interested them "because they're a becoming and aren't part of history" (Negotiations 153).

Approaches that come close

Nomadology, the name · back

Several approaches outside Deleuze and Guattari's orbit come close to nomadology. Rather than grade them, each row ends on the question that would turn its concept toward a nomadology. The letters point to the threads on the front page. The numbers point to the guiding problems on the back of the sheet on royal and nomad science.

Other names

Nomadology, the name · names

The names already in use, what a new name would need, and the routes one could take. Candidate words are left to the author.

Names already in use

  • Challenger's six others. Rhizomatics, stratoanalysis, schizoanalysis, micropolitics, pragmatics, the science of multiplicities 43
  • Geophilosophy. Deleuze and Guattari's own later name, for a philosophy that arises from "a geography rather than a historiography" (What Is Philosophy? 96). They credit Nietzsche with founding it (102)
  • The 1977 aliases. Diagrammatism and cartography, which the 1980 list drops (Dialogues 125)
  • Geopoetics. Kenneth White's name, since the late 1970s, for "cette densification de la géographie" ("this densification of geography") that the nomad must invent (L'Esprit nomade 11, 272-94). A rival name in use, with the nomad kept outside politics
  • Minor science, nomad science. The science that follows its material, against royal science 361-74. It is the name of this site
  • Nomad thought. The title of Deleuze's 1972 talk, and of the thought set against the State form 376-80
  • Mobile pastoralism. What steppe research now calls the way of life once named nomadism (Humphrey and Sneath). It names an economy, not a method

What a new name would need

  1. Name no people, because a people was never the root
  2. Keep distribution without division, the pasture no one parcels out
  3. Keep the opened unit and the local link, step by step
  4. Put politics before being, as Guattari did ("before Being there is politics," Dialogues 17) and the plateaus repeat ("avant l'être, il y a la politique," "before being, there is politics," Mille plateaux 249)
  5. Avoid ending in a law or a logos, or make that ending visible on purpose
  6. Be sayable outside the university

Where one could come from

  • From history (A). Named for geography against history, the standpoint ATP 23 asks for (Dialogues 2, 23). Deleuze and Guattari took this route themselves in 1991, with geophilosophy
  • From noology (B). Named for a thought without image, against the State form in thought (Dialogues 13-14, ATP 376)
  • From the opened monad (C). Named for the local, step-by-step link that replaces the closed unit 494, 574 n. 27
  • From the nomads (D). Named for a way of life without a people, as Laroche's adjective nomádes was 121
  • From nem- (E). Named for distributing rather than dividing, the pasture no one parcels out 380
  • From the word itself (F). Take one of the aliases, add an eighth as the joke allows 43, or keep "nomadology" and tell its history

Our own observation, which Deleuze and Guattari do not make, is that "nomado-logy" puts a logos over the nomos. Plateau 12 calls this move "the triumph of the logos or the law over the nomos" 373.
